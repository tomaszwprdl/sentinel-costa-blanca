# COPY-DISCIPLINE-CODEX.md  
Sentinel — Copy Discipline Codex  

**Status:** Active launch-era guidance (PL + EN)

Applies to all user-facing copy. Service or legal substance changes require Owner approval.

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines linguistic discipline for all Sentinel communication.

It governs:

- Sentence structure  
- Word selection  
- Tone constraints  
- Disclosure behavior  
- FAQ construction  
- PL / EN alignment rules  

It does not define service scope (see SERVICE-STRUCTURING.md).  
It does not define layout (see `LAYOUT COMPOSITION.md`).  
It does not define brand philosophy (see DECISIONS.md / BRAND.md).  

This document defines how Sentinel speaks.

If copy weakens structural authority, it is invalid.

---

# 2. Foundational Tone Principle

**Copy may be clearer, warmer, and more human, but must not become promotional, vague, or concierge-like.**

Sentinel language should be:

- Procedural and boundary-aware  
- Structured and measured  
- Direct for remote owners  

Avoid:

- Empty reassurance and lifestyle framing  
- Promotional hype and vague “full support” claims  
- Concierge or hospitality vocabulary  

Clarity prevails over charm — warmth is fine when it aids clarity, not when it replaces structure.

---

# 3. Sentence Architecture Rules

## 3.1 Structural Preference

Prefer:

- Declarative sentences  
- Parameter-based language  
- Quantified statements  
- Defined limits  

Avoid:

- Open-ended emotional framing  
- Narrative storytelling  
- Rhetorical questions  

---

## 3.2 Length Discipline

- Maximum 2–3 sentences per paragraph block  
- Prefer 12–22 words per sentence  
- Avoid chained subordinate clauses  

If explanation requires more:
→ use structured bullets.

---

## 3.3 Active Structure

Prefer:

- “Sentinel performs monthly inspections.”
- “Owner approval is required above the defined limit.”

Avoid:

- “We try to ensure…”
- “You can feel confident that…”

---

# 4. Allowed Linguistic Constructs

## 4.1 Structural Language

Allowed patterns:

- “Defined within contract.”
- “Minimum guaranteed frequency.”
- “Autonomous decision limit.”
- “Operational allocation.”
- “Jurisdiction boundary.”

---

## 4.2 Authority Framing

Allowed:

- “Sentinel acts within defined limits.”
- “Execution occurs after owner confirmation.”
- “Escalation follows documented procedure.”

---

## 4.3 Disclosure Language

Allowed:

- “View full scope”
- “See structural breakdown”
- “Detailed parameters available”

Not allowed:

- “Learn more about how we care”
- “Discover how we support you”


---

## 4.4 Operational Capability Language

Keyholding and cleaning are permitted terms.

They must be framed as operational capabilities inside a structured oversight system.

Allowed:

- "Keyholding is handled within the agreed scope."
- "Cleaning / readiness cleaning may be included according to usage pathway."
- "Turnover cleaning support is available for Active Guest Use."
- "Access coordination is documented."

Not allowed:

- "We are your cleaning service."
- "We take care of everything."
- "Complete rental management."
- "Holiday home care."

Operational capability copy must state scope, frequency, or condition where relevant.

- klient od pustego mieszkania

EN:

- Użycie mieszane / nieustalone

EN:

# 6. Parameter Expression Rules

When presenting limits:

- Use numeric expression where possible.
- Avoid vague qualifiers.

Prefer:
- “Response within 24h.”
- “Minimum 2 inspections per month.”

Avoid:
- “Fast response.”
- “Regular visits.”

---

# 7. FAQ Construction Rules

FAQ answers must:

- Define boundary
- State procedure
- Clarify limitation
- Avoid reassurance tone

Structure:

1. Direct answer.
2. Condition or limit.
3. Procedural clarification (if required).

Example (EN):

Q: Does Sentinel handle legal compliance?

A:
Sentinel does not provide legal advice.  
Regulatory compliance remains the responsibility of the owner.  
Operational oversight may identify observable inconsistencies.

---

# 8. PL / EN Parity Rule

Every structural concept must exist in both languages.

Cursor must:

- Update PL and EN simultaneously.
- Preserve parameter clarity in both.
- Avoid literal translation if clarity degrades.

If mismatch occurs:
→ Preserve structure over elegance.
Owner will review refinement.

---

# 9. CTA Discipline

CTAs must be:

- Neutral
- Structural
- Action-specific

Allowed:

- “Request structured review”
- “View package comparison”
- “Initiate onboarding”

Not allowed:

- “Get started today”
- “Secure your peace of mind”
- “Join now”

---

# 10. Numerics & Formatting

- Use consistent units (h, month, €).
- Avoid approximate phrasing unless legally required.
- Financial thresholds: defined, not implied.

---

# 11. Conflict Resolution Rule

If copy conflicts with:

- DECISIONS.md → DECISIONS prevails.
- SERVICE-STRUCTURING.md → Service document prevails.
- LAYOUT-COMPOSITION.md → Layout prevails structurally.

Copy must adapt.  
Copy never overrides structure.

---

# 12. Enforcement Principle

If text:

- Adds emotional weight
- Softens authority
- Removes boundaries
- Introduces ambiguity

It must be rewritten.

---

# 13. Governance Note

Material changes to copy discipline require Owner approval. Routine PL/EN copy edits do not require STATUS.md or AI-GOVERNANCE.md updates unless they change protected positioning, service meaning, or prohibited-language policy.
