## 1. Purpose

This document defines:

- Service architecture  
- Package differentiation logic  
- Operational modes  
- Pricing structure  
- Jurisdiction limits  
- Execution boundaries  

This document governs WHAT Sentinel offers and HOW it scales.

It does not define layout (see LAYOUT-COMPOSITION.md).  
It does not define tone (see BRAND.md).  

---

## 2. Core Identity Constraint

Sentinel is:

> A structured local oversight system.

Sentinel is not:

- A concierge service  
- A property management platform  
- A cleaning company  

All service structuring decisions must preserve this identity.

If a feature weakens oversight positioning → reject.

---

## 3. Escalation Integrity Principle (Binding)

Packages may differ ONLY by:

1. Level of autonomous decision authority  
2. Inspection intensity (minimum guaranteed frequency)  
3. Operational priority / allocation  

Packages must not differ by lifestyle convenience.

Future modifications must preserve this escalation axis.

---

## 4. Service Architecture Overview

### Two Structural Layers

**Layer 1 — Oversight Packages (Primary Offering)**  
Jurisdiction + inspection + decision framework.

**Layer 2 — Operational Support (Secondary / Non-Preferred)**  
Execution-only services without structured oversight.

Layer 2 must never redefine Layer 1.

---

## 5. Oversight Packages

Three escalating levels.

Working names (subject to brand-layer refinement):

- Structured Presence  
- Active Oversight  
- Extended Jurisdiction  

> NOTE FOR BRAND.md: Polish equivalents require refinement to avoid bureaucratic tone.

---

## 6. Package Parameters (Structural Minimum)

Each package defines:

- Minimum inspections per month  
- Autonomous emergency decision limit  
- Response priority (SLA)  
- Operational allocation level  
- Estimated monthly range (final amount)

---

### 6.1 Structured Presence

Profile:
Low intensity, stable property, minimal operational activity.

Includes:
- Minimum 1 inspection / month  
- Low emergency decision limit  
- Response within 48h  
- Basic structured report  
- Vendor coordination (external cost)

Purpose:
Maintain structural integrity of property.

---

### 6.2 Active Oversight

Profile:
Primary segment. Balanced risk awareness.

Includes:
- Minimum 2 inspections / month  
- Medium emergency decision limit  
- Response within 24h  
- Seasonal readiness check  
- Limited contractor supervision  
- Structured reporting

Purpose:
Operational stability and risk reduction.

Target distribution:
Primary expected selection (≈ majority).

---

### 6.3 Extended Jurisdiction

Profile:
High intensity property, high risk tolerance avoidance.

Includes:
- Minimum 2 inspections / month  
- High emergency decision limit  
- Same-day response window (within operational hours)
- Accelerated physical presence window (package-defined)
- Priority channel access  
- Extended infrastructure checklist  
- Pre- and post-arrival readiness protocol  
- Escalation priority

Purpose:
Priority operational allocation and reduced decision burden.

Premium differentiation based on:
- Time allocation  
- Access  
- Operational priority  

Not concierge.

---

## 7. Inspection Logic (Hybrid Model)

Inspection frequency is defined as:

> Minimum guaranteed inspections.

Operational flexibility allowed beyond minimum where required.

This preserves:

- Clarity for client  
- Operational realism for Sentinel  

---

## 8. Emergency Decision Model

Each package includes:

- Fixed autonomous spending limit  
- Owner authorization required above limit  
- Safety-critical exceptions defined contractually  

Model:
Fixed limit + owner escalation (Model C).

---

## 9. Operational Mode Overlay

Two modes:

- Residential Mode (default)  
- Active Guest Mode  

Mode does not change package level.

Mode adjusts operational intensity.

---

### 9.1 Residential Mode

Standard oversight allocation.  
No event-driven coordination.

---

### 9.2 Active Guest Mode

Includes:

- Operational readiness allocation  
- Turnover coordination (per event billing)  
- Cleaning coordination (execution billed separately)  
- Damage verification checks  
- Increased event-driven communication  

Does NOT:

- Change decision limit  
- Change SLA category  
- Include physical labor in package  

Guest Mode pricing model:

- Additional fixed monthly operational allocation  
- Per-event execution billing  

---

## 10. Regulatory Neutrality Clause

Sentinel:

- Does not provide legal advice  
- Does not verify rental licensing status  
- Does not act as regulatory authority  

Legal compliance remains sole responsibility of property owner.

Sentinel's role is limited to operational oversight.

---

## 11. Pricing Communication Framework

Public display:

- Estimated monthly range (final amount)  
- Range reflects Residential baseline  

Guest Mode adjustment displayed via switch.

Final pricing confirmed after structured review.

No "starting from".  
No hidden escalation.

---

## 12. Disclosure Principle

Detailed scope must be accessible via single click.

Disclosure used to:

- Preserve clarity in main layout  
- Allow deep review without visual overload  

Disclosure cannot conceal core parameters.

---

## 13. Operational Support (Without Oversight)

Separate section.  
Not equal to packages.

Available for clients requiring execution-only services.

Includes:

- Cleaning coordination  
- Key holding  
- Event-based assistance  

Excludes:

- Structured oversight  
- Inspection framework  
- Autonomous decision authority  
- SLA commitment  

Clear limitation of responsibility must be stated.

Minimum engagement period:
3 months.

Billing:
Monthly in advance.

Rationale:
Operational continuity and structural stability.
