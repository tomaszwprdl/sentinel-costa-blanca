## TOP 5 BRAND RISKS (RANKED)

1. **Hero and final CTA tone (Home)** — "Opieka pakietowa", "Bez zobowiązań", "Gotowy zabezpieczyć?", "Rozpocznij"/"Get Started" introduce hospitality, reassurance, and conversion CTA language. BRAND §4.3 and COPY CODEX §5.1 / §9 explicitly prohibit these. Highest visibility; sets first and last impression.
2. **Package naming and hero framing around "opieka"** — "Opieka nad Nieruchomością", "Opieka + Dostęp" and hero "Opieka pakietowa" repeat a prohibited term and hospitality framing across Home and Services, anchoring the brand in "care" rather than oversight/representation.
3. **Three-column package comparison (Services)** — Side-by-side Green | Orange | Red table conflicts with DECISIONS §23 (vertical containment; no three-column SaaS layout). Encourages product-comparison mental model and startup/SaaS feel.
4. **Problem framing as "Zarządzanie" (Home)** — "Wyzwanie Zdalnego Zarządzania Nieruchomością" frames the category as property management. DECISIONS §3 defines Sentinel as not generic property management; this blurs representation/oversight vs zarządzanie.
5. **EN conversion and personalization copy** — "Ready to secure your property?", "Get Started", "personalized quote" breach COPY CODEX and EN-ADAPTATION (no "Get started today", no reassurance, no comfort/personalization). Structural EN parity is present but tone drifts in high-traffic CTAs and contact CTA.
