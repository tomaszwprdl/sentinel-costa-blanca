# COGNITIVE-PAGE-AUDIT.md — SENTINEL

**Status:** Optional audit tool — not doctrine, not a binding contract.

Use on **one page at a time**. For binding rules, read `doc/DECISIONS.md`, `doc/SERVICE-STRUCTURING.md`, `doc/COPY-DISCIPLINE-CODEX.md`, `doc/VISUAL-FREEDOM-SPRINT.md`, and `doc/AGENT-BRIEFING.md` first. This instrument does not override them.

---

# Instrument purpose

Aggressive structural stress test for a single Sentinel page.

Determine whether the page:

- Clearly defines the operational problem
- Structurally demonstrates the mechanism
- Defines boundaries and responsibility
- Reduces perceived personal exposure
- Feels inevitable to a rational remote owner
- Aligns with Sentinel service boundaries and launch-era visual direction

Evaluate visual structure **only where it affects** comprehension, operational proof, trust, scanability, or perceived local credibility. **Do not critique taste for its own sake.**

No compliments. No politeness filler. No generic advice.

---

# Simulated reader

Remote property owner:

- Risk-aware, legally literate, time-constrained
- Suspicious of vagueness and hidden flexibility
- Allergic to concierge softness and overpromising locals
- Afraid of being trapped in undefined scope

You are hunting structural weakness, not surface polish.

---

# Non-negotiable audit rules

For every claim → demand mechanism.  
For every benefit → demand boundary.  
For every strong statement → attempt to attack it.

If you cannot challenge it, it passes.  
If you can construct doubt, it fails.

---

# Evidence (screenshots)

Screenshots are **optional evidence** for page audits.

When used, prefer current captures on demand:

- Desktop full page
- Mobile full page (390px width minimum)
- Relevant section close-ups (hero, proof modules, packages, CTAs, disclosures)

Do **not** treat old screenshots, archived PDFs, or build-era captures as current evidence.

---

# Launch-era visual context

Build-era “no visual experimentation” rules are retired. This audit **allows**:

- Warmer surfaces and richer rhythm
- Image-led sections where they aid recognition
- Stronger cards and proof-led modules
- Local operational context and more attractive atmosphere

This audit **rejects**:

- Concierge drift
- Rental-management drift
- Luxury villa / resort / tourism energy
- Fake testimonials or fake proof
- Generic SaaS dashboards with no operational function
- Vague “we handle everything” copy
- Softened service boundaries

Warmer presentation passes when it improves clarity and local credibility. Warmth that replaces structure fails.

---

# Phase 1 — Problem clarity & escalation

**Test:**

- Is operational pain explicitly defined?
- Is it causal, not merely descriptive?
- Does it model what goes wrong over time?
- Does it show why ad-hoc / DIY management fails structurally?
- Is cost of delay visible?
- Is there contrast: managed vs unmanaged?

If pain is informational but not escalating → **FAIL**.

**Output:** PASS / WEAK / FAIL

---

# Phase 2 — Mechanism verification

For every major claim, ask:

- Where is the structural mechanism?
- Where is responsibility defined, bounded, enforced?

Examples:

- “Defined responsibility” → where defined? where bounded?
- “Reduced risk” → what specifically reduces it?

Any benefit without mechanism → credibility leak.

**Output:** PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL

---

# Phase 3 — Expectation resolution

For each strong statement:

- What expectation does it create?
- Is that expectation resolved on this page?

Deferred expectation without explanation → expectation leak.

**Output:** RESOLVED / LEAK PRESENT

---

# Phase 4 — Brand & service-boundary alignment

Test against Sentinel launch-era doctrine:

- Institutional clarity, not personality-led charm
- Bounded responsibility, not open-ended care
- Structured oversight, not concierge hospitality
- Operational company presence, not policy PDF tone
- Qualification over conversion pressure

Flag:

- Softness without structure
- Concierge / lifestyle / rental-management drift
- Marketing exaggeration
- Interpretive language (“reasonable”, “probably”, “flexible” without limits)

**Output:** NONE / MINOR / MODERATE / CRITICAL

---

# Phase 5 — Risk signal detection

Simulate skepticism. Where would a cautious owner hesitate?

Check for unresolved:

- Financial ambiguity
- Authority ambiguity
- Scope ambiguity
- Emergency ambiguity
- Price opacity
- Interpretive terms
- Over-rigidity perception

For each signal: dissolved later on page, or left open?

---

# Phase 6 — DIY illusion neutralization

Construct strongest resistance:

- “I’ll manage this myself.”
- “I’ll call someone when needed.”
- “This seems too rigid.”
- “This feels heavy.”

Does the page neutralize structurally?

**Output:** NEUTRALIZED / NOT NEUTRALIZED  
**DIY resistance strength:** Low / Medium / High

---

# Phase 7 — Cognitive load & scanability

Simulate a 4-minute reader.

- Vertical escalation (problem → mechanism → boundary → action)?
- Or horizontal stacking and repetition?
- Scannable without rereading?
- Conceptual duplication?
- ~20% compression opportunity without loss?

**Output:** EFFICIENT / DENSE / FATIGUING

---

# Phase 8 — Skeptical interruption simulation

After each major section:

- What objection forms here?
- Does the page dissolve it?

List top 3 objections the page fails to dissolve.

---

# Phase 9 — 60-second transfer test

After one read, can the reader explain Sentinel:

- Clearly
- Accurately
- With boundaries
- Without vagueness

**Output:** TRANSFERABLE / NOT TRANSFERABLE

---

# Phase 10 — No-Sentinel world contrast

Can the reader visualize unmanaged exposure?

Weak contrast → weak urgency.

**Output:** STRONG CONTRAST / WEAK CONTRAST

---

# Phase 11 — Launch-era visual proof & local credibility

Evaluate structure and visuals **only for comprehension, proof, trust, scanability, local credibility**:

| Check | Pass criterion |
|-------|----------------|
| Local operating company | Feels like a real on-site operator, not a remote platform or policy document |
| Proof timing | Operational proof visible early enough to earn trust before long prose |
| Prose vs mechanism | Page does not rely on explanation where proof/structure should carry load |
| Visual evidence | Reports, access, area, process, or documentation shown — not only described |
| Warmth vs drift | Warmth improves clarity; does not slide into lifestyle/concierge |
| Imagery role | Images support operations; not decoration or resort fantasy |
| Policy PDF feel | Boundaries remain clear while page feels less like a compliance PDF |
| CTA discipline | CTAs visible and purposeful; not conversion-pressure or package bullying |

Note visual issues only when they harm the above. Decorative choices that aid clarity pass.

**Output:** STRONG / ADEQUATE / WEAK / FAILING

---

# Phase 12 — Inevitability final gate

Does Sentinel feel:

- A logical conclusion?
- The structured alternative?
- The rational default?

Or merely one option among many?

Construct strongest remaining resistance argument. Rate its strength.

If resistance stays strong → inevitability failure.

**Inevitability index:** 0–10

---

# Final core check

The page succeeds only if a rational remote owner finishes and concludes:

> “This is structured. This reduces my exposure. This is bounded. This is predictable. I understand what I am entering.”

If that conclusion is not inevitable → refinement required.
