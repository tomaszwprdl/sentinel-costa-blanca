# BRAND.md — SENTINEL

Brand meaning and communication posture. Launch-era guidance.

**Brand discipline protects meaning, not dullness.**

Sentinel may feel warmer, more tactile, more premium, and more visually attractive if service boundaries remain clear.

The website should feel like a **real local operating company**, not a policy document.

Current launch state: Services, estimator, FAQ, About, and Contact are shipped/live and protected unless the Owner explicitly reopens them. The next brand risk is not weak design; it is over-polished / synthetic trust leakage caused by fake proof, buried price logic, repetitive defensive language, and repeated motifs after they stop clarifying.

---

## 1. Core identity

Sentinel is structured property oversight through defined authority and documented process.

- Local operating company, not a remote platform brand
- Bounded responsibility, not open-ended care
- Procedure and documentation over persuasion

**PL:** Reprezentacja właściciela na miejscu  
**EN:** Structured Property Oversight

---

## 2. What Sentinel is not

- Concierge or lifestyle support
- Rental management or guest-hospitality brand
- Generic property management
- Cleaning-only or keyholding-only company

Keyholding and cleaning are **capabilities inside oversight**, not the public category.

---

## 3. Moral position

Responsible limitation:

- Authority bounded and documented
- Scope explicit; financial limits predefined
- Real local presence, not symbolic reassurance

Clarity and process matter more than charm — but clarity can be delivered in clear, human language.

---

## 4. Communication posture

**Prefer:**

- Scope, access, escalation, response windows
- Operational facts and boundaries
- Owner-facing directness

**Avoid:**

- Peace-of-mind clichés and worry-free promises
- Luxury, resort, or white-glove vocabulary
- “We handle everything” vagueness
- Fake proof or testimonial theater

Copy may be clearer, warmer, and more human without becoming promotional or concierge-like. See `COPY-DISCIPLINE-CODEX.md`.

---

## 5. Visual posture (website)

**Brand discipline protects meaning, not dullness.** Visual excellence is allowed.

The site uses **one art-directed Sentinel atmosphere** (merged on `main`) — not a policy document, not a generic SaaS skin, not a toggleable light/dark app theme.

**Allowed when service boundaries stay clear:**

- Warmth, depth, cinematic surfaces, image-led composition
- Premium editorial treatment, tactile property context, controlled accent system
- Full-section or full-page composition changes when Owner-scoped

**Still weak:**

- Decoration without operational function
- Fake proof, testimonial theater, lifestyle/concierge drift
- Synthetic images treated as real evidence
- Repeated keys, folders, dossiers, doors, and limits language after they stop clarifying the service

**Responsive IA divergence is allowed.** Mobile may reorder and compress a page into a buyer-oriented flow (e.g. Services mobile leading with the estimator) while desktop stays in dossier/specification form. Same meaning and boundaries, different density. This is a usability decision, not a brand weakening, as long as service boundaries and protected contracts stay intact. See `DECISIONS.md` "Responsive IA".

Logo and wordmark integrity remain protected by `DECISIONS.md`. Logo restrictions apply to the **logo asset**, not every background, card, or accent on the page.

Current visual state and next work: `STATUS.md` and `TASK.md`.

Current proof-layer images are allowed only as temporary shot-planning placeholders. They must not claim actual Sentinel work, real client property, real reports, real operator identity, or final operational evidence. Replacement with real photography remains a pre-promotion / indexed-launch goal where possible.

Contact-specific visual lock:

- Contact uses the Night Desk / controlled intake model.
- The form is a crisp qualification dossier inside a dark secure environment.
- The hero may use `public/photos/contact-night-window.webp` as a visible but subdued architectural/lit-window background.
- Do not revert Contact to beige paperwork.
- Do not add casual contact imagery, call-center visuals, beach/lifestyle imagery, testimonials, or sales urgency.
